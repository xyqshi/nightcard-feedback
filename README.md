# NightCard feedback

Bug reports and feature requests for **NightCard**, the bedtime scheduler and remote for Yoto players on iPhone.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/xyqshi/nightcard-feedback/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
- [Request a feature](https://github.com/xyqshi/nightcard-feedback/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)

You can also open these from the app: **Settings → Feedback**. The app fills in its version and your iOS version for you.

Everything posted here is public. Please don't include your email address, your Yoto account details or anything else personal. If you'd rather write privately, email info@cortexai-tech.com.

NightCard is an independent app by Cortex AI LLC and is not affiliated with Yoto Ltd.
